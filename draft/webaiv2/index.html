ABOUT THE STUDY

The data behind
the story.

The analysis uses approximately 5.1 million eligible pages in 71 Common Crawl snapshots from January 2019 through August 2026. The outcome is text generated or edited using language models. Estimates are weighted to eligible pages and include model-based uncertainty.

Open Pangram scored eligible pages; Binoculars was also applied to English pages. The model uses labeled benchmarks and earlier controls to account for detector error. The results describe crawlable pages with at least 500 characters of extracted text.

Read data and methods notes

Credible intervals are 95% intervals. Domains are grouped by PageRank in Common Crawl’s link graph, not by the number of people who read them. Language categories are English only, no English detected, and English plus another language; pages of unknown language are not included in those three series. The model does not explicitly include additional similarity among pages on the same domain.

Data come from the matched sp_full model run. Earlier crawls inform estimates of false-positive rates under an assumption of low AI prevalence. Common-composition estimates use model predictions under a shared mix of page characteristics.
